Self-Reflective Reading: Authentication is Paramount
Introduction 

In today's digital landscape, user authentication security is paramount, especially as cyber threats become increasingly sophisticated. One of the most promising approaches to enhance security is using biometrics, which leverages unique biological characteristics of individuals—such as fingerprints, facial recognition, and iris scans—to verify identities. This literature review explores various applications of biometric technology in securing cyber systems, highlighting examples beyond those discussed in the lectures.

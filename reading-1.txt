Self-Reflective Reading: Hands-On ML Examples and Development Process
Introduction

In this module, we explore foundational concepts in Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML), with a focus on building an understanding before diving into AI applications specific to cybersecurity. The first lecture introduces the basics of AI, its applications, and the potential benefits it can bring to cyber analysis. The second lecture covers the three types of Machine Learning (ML): 

Supervised Learning

Unsupervised Learning

Reinforcement Learning

Hands-on examples in this module involve supervised linear regression, neural networks, and unsupervised clustering, offering practical exposure to the ML analytic development process.

Task Overview
For this module's discussion, your tasks will include:

Summarizing the three types of Machine Learning: Briefly explain the differences between supervised learning, unsupervised learning, and reinforcement learning.

Review and Develop Hands-On ML Examples: Work through the supervised linear regression, neural network, and unsupervised clustering examples.

Use the language and steps from the ML analytic development process to summarize the development of each ML example.

Examine the datasets before they are fed into the classifiers. Determine whether the features are raw or transformed.

Attempt to optimize the parameters to improve the results for each example, such as improving the alignment in the linear regression model.

Advised Steps
Summarize the Machine Learning Types:

Understand how supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning differ in terms of data, labels, and learning goals.

Review ML Analytic Development Process:

Understand the steps involved in the ML process, from data collection to training and evaluation.

Summarize the process for the given examples and note how the models are developed and tested.

Assess Data Preparation:

Investigate the data being used for training and testing. Determine whether the data is raw or transformed (e.g., scaled, normalized).

Optimization of Models:

Experiment with optimizing model parameters such as the number of iterations, learning rates, or neural network layers to achieve better performance.

Self-Reflective Questions
Understanding ML Types:

How do the differences between supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning influence the types of problems they can solve?

Which type of learning do you think is most commonly applied in cybersecurity, and why?

ML Analytic Development Process:

What challenges did you face in following the ML analytic development process for the examples provided?

How does the development process ensure that the models are robust and generalizable?

Data Preparation and Feature Transformation:

Why is it important to understand whether features have been transformed or not before feeding them into classifiers?

What impact does data transformation (e.g., scaling or normalization) have on the performance of ML models?

Optimization of Parameters:

What specific changes did you make when optimizing the models, and how did it affect the results?

How could the optimization of these models be applied to real-world cybersecurity problems to enhance performance?

By working through these tasks, you will not only deepen your understanding of machine learning fundamentals but also reflect on the process of applying these techniques, preparing you for more advanced AI applications in cybersecurity.
